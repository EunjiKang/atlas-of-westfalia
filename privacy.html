Privacy Policy

This notice describes the GitHub edition of Atlas of Westfalia. Contact: hi@atlasofwestfalia.com. This mailbox is planned and must be activated before public launch.

Stories stay with you

Story text, approximate places, Instagram credits, saved selections and your bag are stored in your browser. They are not sent to the Atlas team. Uploaded photo previews are held only in the open page and disappear on reload or close. Images are redrawn for preview, removing embedded metadata. The site does not request your device location.

Local data remains until you delete it using the site’s saved-story controls or clear this site’s browser data. Export writing you wish to keep. Other people using the same browser profile may see it. Clearing storage can permanently remove local stories.

Email and notifications

Email registration is not connected in this edition. No subscription or email delivery occurs. Saving a story displays an on-screen confirmation only; it does not send an email to you or the Atlas team. If email enquiries are enabled later, your email app will let you review and send the message. We will use enquiries to respond, and any requested updates only with your agreement. You may ask us to stop updates or delete correspondence; records needed for unresolved enquiries or legal obligations may need to be retained.

Payments

Checkout is closed. This site collects no card or bank details. Any future PayPal payment will take place on PayPal’s own site under its privacy notice; purchase and receipt details will be handled by the seller and payment provider.

Hosting and external services

The default atlas uses local assets. GitHub Pages, when used to host this site, receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Choosing Street map loads map data from OpenFreeMap and may expose your IP address and requested map area to that service. External Instagram, source and payment links connect to their respective sites when opened. No analytics or advertising tracking is added by this edition.

Optional ChatGPT assistant

If an Atlas assistant link is enabled, it opens ChatGPT in a new tab. Your local stories are not automatically sent there. Messages you choose to enter are handled by ChatGPT under its own privacy terms; the assistant cannot save stories to this website or manage your payments.

Your choices

You can use the offline-style map, avoid external links, delete local drafts and clear browser data. Contact us about published material or correspondence; we cannot retrieve or erase the private drafts stored only on your device. This notice must be updated before remote submissions, email registration or payments are enabled.
