# Publish the visitor assistant

A public GPT URL has not been created. The builder could not be accessed through this session.

1. Open ChatGPT’s GPT builder in your own account (https://chatgpt.com/gpts/editor). Availability depends on your account.
2. Name it “Atlas of Westfalia — Story Companion”. Use the instructions in ATLAS-ASSISTANT.md. Suggested description: “A friendly guide to van memories, Tiny Turn and the Atlas of Westfalia.”
3. Use the four conversation starters in that file. No custom actions or payment connections are needed.
4. Use the builder’s sharing controls to allow public link access if offered by your account. Review the displayed access setting, then copy the generated GPT URL. Do not use a shared conversation URL as a substitute.
5. Test from another account and, if important to you, signed out. Public sharing is not a promise of anonymous or unlimited access.
6. Paste the generated https://chatgpt.com/g/g-… URL into assistant-config.js, retaining the quotation marks, and upload that file again. The site’s footer link will appear.

The assistant cannot access visitors’ local drafts, charge PayPal, cancel orders or email the team. Do not put credentials, private photos, addresses or unpublished personal information in its knowledge files.
